A 2D tower defense game made with godot

## About

Enemies walk down a road towards your base and you have to stop them before they get there. Place towers next to the road, kill enemies to earn smiles, then spend those smiles on more towers and upgrades. If an enemy gets through you lose base health equal to however much health it had left, so a nearly dead enemy hurts way less than a full health one. Some enemies are camo or metal so you cant just spam one tower and win. Beat all the waves or try endless mode and see how far you can get. The game saves by itself between waves so you can quit and come back later.

## How To Play

- Click a tower in the sidebar to pick it up (or press its number key)
- Left Click - To place the tower (green means you can place it, red means you cant)
- Right Click or Q - To cancel placing a tower
- Click a placed tower - To open its upgrade panel (it also shows the towers range)
- Click anywhere else - To close the upgrade panel
- Space - To start the next wave
- Lock button - Shows up when you select a tower that can lock its aim (machine gunner, and the bomb after the bounce upgrade)

The next wave only starts when every enemy is dead.

## Towers

- Tower -- Your basic tower. Shoots enemies that walk into its range
- Xbow -- Shoots arrows at enemies in range
- Bomb -- Explodes on hit and damages every enemy in a small circle. Can hurt metal enemies. With the bounce upgrade the bombs keep hopping around the screen and explode at every landing, and you can lock where they land
- Sniper -- Infinite range, always hits the enemy that is furthest along the road. Slow but hits hard and can see camo from the start. Cant hurt metal until you buy the piercing upgrade
- Machine Gunner -- Shoots really fast and aims at your mouse. Press Lock then click on the map to make it aim at one spot, press Unlock to go back to following your mouse. It only fires while an enemy is in its range though
- Ace -- A plane that flies in circles around its base and fires a fan of darts. The darts fly straight like the real bloons ace so they can miss, unless you buy dart tracking
- Furnace Tower -- Throws flames onto the road near it. Each flame breaks after hitting a few enemies or after a while
- Wizard -- Spawns minions
- Supporter (currently unavailable) -- Basically buffs the stats of nearby towers

## Upgrades

Click on a tower and a panel pops up. Each upgrade has 3 levels and costs smiles, the button greys out if you cant afford it. Every tower type has its own panel:

- Damage -- Hits harder
- Fire Rate -- Shoots faster
- Range -- Bigger range (not on the sniper or the ace)
- Accuracy -- Machine gunner only, makes the bullets spread out less
- Piercing -- Sniper and spike tower only, lets them hurt metal enemies
- Bounce -- Bomb only, the bombs keep bouncing off the edges of the screen for a few seconds. Only 3 bombs can be bouncing at once so the tower waits until one runs out. It also unlocks the Lock button for the bomb
- Dart Tracking -- Ace only, the darts turn towards enemies near them
- Target -- Switches between First, Last, Strongest and Weakest
- Sell -- Gives you back half of everything you spent on that tower

## Targeting

- First -- The enemy that is furthest along the road
- Last -- The enemy that is furthest back
- Strongest -- The enemy with the most health
- Weakest -- The enemy with the least health

## Smiles

You get smiles from killing enemies and a green number pops up where the enemy died. When you spend smiles a red number shows up next to your mouse. Selling a tower gives you smiles back too. They are basically the games currency.

## Damage Types

- Sharp -- Used by most towers. Bounces off metal enemies
- Explosive -- Used by the bomb. Hurts metal enemies
- Piercing -- Used by the sniper and spikes after the upgrade. Hurts metal enemies

## Enemies

### Normal Enemies -- Walk down the road and try to reach your base

- Verity -> Just a normal enemy
- Falsity -> A fast enemy
- Tankity -> A slow tank with lots of health
- Cruelty -> A fast tank

### Pack Enemies -- Come in groups of 5

- Lovity -> A pack of 5 normal speed enemies
- Moggity -> A pack of 5 fast enemies

## Special Enemies

Any enemy can also be one of these, or even both at the same time

- Camo -- See through enemies. Towers cant shoot them unless they can see camo (the sniper can from the start)
- Metal -- Blue looking enemies. Sharp damage bounces off them so you need bombs or piercing

## Game Modes

- Play -- You will get the options to choose between maps and you will play with a certain wave number
- Endless -- Waves never stop. Every wave has more enemies, more health and new enemy types show up as you go. The game ends when your base runs out of health and it shows you what wave you reached

## Saving

The game saves by itself when a wave is over and no enemies are left. Your smiles, base health, wave and every tower with its upgrades get saved. Next time you open the game press Continue on the main menu and you pick up from where you left off. If you quit in the middle of a wave you go back to the start of that wave. Dying or beating the last wave deletes the save.

## Game Over

When your base health hits 0 the game freezes and a game over screen fades in with the wave you got to. You can restart or go back to the main menu.
